New here?

What to expect on Sunday

No pressure and no dress code. Here's what a Sunday morning at Prairie Valley looks like.

Worship

Our service starts at 10:45 AM with gospel-centered music, a mix of hymns and contemporary songs, and biblical teaching.

The teaching comes straight from the Bible, usually verse by verse through whole books.

Kids

Kids join us for the first part of the service and sing with us. After a short children's sermon, kids up to 4th grade can head to Children's Church.

Prefer to keep your child with you for the whole service? That's totally fine.

What to wear

Wear what's comfortable. Some folks dress up, most come casual. You'll fit in either way.

We serve a light breakfast every Sunday morning, from donuts to homemade breakfast items. Come feed your belly and your soul.

Visiting with kids? Fill out the guest registration form ahead of time and check-in will be quick and simple when you arrive.

Ministries

Find your place

Real community happens between Sundays too. Here's what's going on through the week.

Sundays · 10:45 AM

Children's Ministry

Kids join the service for singing and a short children's message, then head to Children's Church (up to 4th grade) while the sermon continues.

Wednesdays · 6:00–7:30 PM

Wednesday Night Kids & Student Ministry

A midweek gathering for kids and students to dig into the Bible, build friendships, and have fun. Bible study for all ages happens the same night.

Mondays · 6:00 PM

Small Group Bible Studies

A Ladies' Group and a Men's Group meet on Monday evenings for study, prayer, and real friendship.

Regularly

Men's Ministry

Men gather for prayer meetings and special nights, like our annual Forge Night. Bring a friend.

Regularly

The Lord's Supper

We observe the Lord's Supper together as part of our worship. Check the calendar for the next Sunday.

About us

Who we are

Prairie Valley Church is a church for the entire community in Campbell, Texas. We believe the Bible is God's Word and that the gospel of Jesus Christ is for everyone, so we preach it plainly and welcome anyone who wants to hear it.
